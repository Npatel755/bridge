# 90-second demo

Setup: local stack running, signed in to the demo account, **Load the demo network** done (Home).

1. **Home (5 s).** “What are you working on, Alex?” Tap the example *Who can help prototype a smart bike light?*
2. **Ask results (20 s).** Maya Chen comes first: Direct, quote “works on embedded systems…” from *Your note, Oct 12*. Point at the lime-marked words, the source line, and the suggested ask. Scroll: Priya Natarajan shows *Via Jordan* (possible intro, never promised). Sam Okafor shows an amber *Over a year old* caution. Lee Zhang (follows you) is never a warm route.
3. **Honest no-match (5 s).** Edit question → “Who is a licensed helicopter pilot?” → *No supported match* with next steps.
4. **Note capture (20 s).** Home → Capture a note. Paste: “Coffee with Maya today. Maya loves climbing and is an expert in Rust. Also met Sam Lee who does marketing.” → Find people. Maya matches her card; *Which Sam?* appears for the ambiguous name; skills are checkable. Save to cards.
5. **Build my team (15 s).** Home → Build my team → “Smart bike light with a companion app for commuters” → roles, who fills each, a dashed *Open* role with “Ask your cards who they know”, and *You could help too*.
6. **Network (10 s).** Stack view with letter index; switch to Graph: solid = direct, hollow node = intro path, dashed = unconfirmed/follower.
7. **Nearby simulator (15 s).** Nearby tab → *Run the simulation*. One labeled “simulated for demo” match appears with a coarse band and shared topics; Settings → personas explains why six others were excluded (blocked, no shared interest, off, out of range, poor accuracy, smaller radius).

Closing line: everything shown came from the user's own cards and notes; no AI key was configured and the UI said so.
